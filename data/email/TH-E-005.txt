Subject: Operational Note: TRN-117 (WO-2026-1107-003) zero-traffic workaround

Team,

For work order WO-2026-1107-003 at site OUL-05 (Kiiminki mast):
Field reported that after replacing the SFP on TRN-117 interface 1/1/3, optical link came UP with clear alarms, but traffic remained at zero bytes.

Solution:
On TX-2000 hardware running firmware 4.1 or older:
1. Do not replace optical transceivers or reboot chassis.
2. Disable the interface ('shutdown').
3. Wait 30 seconds for ASIC buffer clearing.
4. Enable the interface ('no shutdown').

This immediately flushes the forwarding table and restores carrier traffic. Please apply this to any similar incidents on 2019-era TX-2000 units.
